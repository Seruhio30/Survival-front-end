# Survival72 — Content Verification Sources

Verification date: 2026-08-11  
Branch: `docs/verify-emergency-content`

## Purpose

This document records the verification of public educational emergency-preparedness content used by Survival72.

Status values:

- `VERIFIED`
- `NEEDS SOURCE`
- `OUTDATED`
- `INCORRECT / MISLEADING`
- `EDITORIAL`

Survival72 prioritizes practical emergency preparedness for Costa Rica. Its main focus is a portable 72-hour emergency backpack that can realistically be carried during an evacuation. A household emergency reserve is treated as a separate concept.

## 10. Editorial principles adopted

1. The primary Survival72 concept is a portable 72-hour emergency backpack that a person can realistically carry.
2. A household emergency reserve is a separate concept and may contain substantially more water, food and supplies.
3. Do not claim that emergency services will arrive within a fixed number of hours.
4. Do not claim that an emergency kit guarantees survival or safety.
5. Prefer actionable guidance over disaster statistics.
6. Statistics must include a clear source, geographic scope, period and definition.
7. Remove statistics that cannot be reliably verified.
8. Prioritize Costa Rican official sources for local guidance.
9. Follow evacuation orders and official alerts over generic preparedness advice.
10. Keep public-facing language clear, practical and non-alarmist.

## Source maintenance

Emergency-preparedness guidance can change.

Before major public presentations, releases or substantial content updates:

1. confirm that the linked official resources are still available;
2. confirm that recommendations have not materially changed;
3. record a new verification date when content is reviewed again;
4. replace broken or superseded official references;
5. avoid copying statistics into public content unless their geographic scope, time period and definition are explicit.
